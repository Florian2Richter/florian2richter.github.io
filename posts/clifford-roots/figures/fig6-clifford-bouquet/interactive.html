A large, gently irregular convex blob, the nine-parameter continuum of unital qubit channels. Through the grey point P inside it, the completely depolarizing channel, runs a bouquet of six thin straight teal needles, the Clifford channels: one sheet per skeleton, with the dials varying continuously along each, all sheets meeting at P. The needles extend further toward positive dials than negative ones, and the identity channel sits at the tip of the identity-skeleton needle, marked id.
